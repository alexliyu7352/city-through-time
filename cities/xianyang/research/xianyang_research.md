Research date: 2026-10-18. Film range: Neolithic → 2025 (one post-2025 item is listed separately as an "echo").
All coordinates WGS84 decimal degrees unless flagged. OSM/Wikipedia/Wikidata are WGS84; nothing here was taken from Baidu/Gaode maps (GCJ-02/BD-09), so no datum offset applies.

Status key used in every row:
- [OK]   verified from an official, academic or major-news source that was actually fetched and quoted
- [SEC]  secondary source only (Wikipedia zh/en, citypopulation.de) or quoted from a search-engine snippet of an official page that could not be opened from this sandbox
- [WEAK] lead only (tourism/aggregator sites); must not be shown on screen without further verification
- [EST]  coordinate estimated by the researcher from a textual description; verify before use

## 3. ANCHOR CANDIDATES (ranked)

Model case: 金沙遗址 (成都) – buried ~3000 BP, found 2001 during construction, 太阳神鸟 became the city emblem.

### Rank 1 — 汉阳陵 + the airport (strongest, best-sourced, and it repeats)
- What spans the time: the tomb of 汉景帝 begun -153 (人民日报海外版: "汉阳陵始建于……公元前153年，营建历时28年"), emperor buried -141; thousands of 1/3-scale terracotta figures in the 外藏坑.
- Dated modern echo: 1990 — "为配合西安咸阳国际机场专用公路建设，陕西省考古研究院汉陵考古队进驻汉阳陵开展考古发掘" → "1990年度全国十大考古新发现"; airport opens 1991-09-01 next door (≈16 km west along the same road); museum 1999; underground pit museum 2006 ("first underground museum in China"); and the echo repeats: 2018 survey for 机场三期 finds 上召窑秦陵, 2020–22 airport dig yields 4,093 tombs ("机场下的古长安", 新华社 2025-02-28), 2025-02 T5 opens, 2026-09 the tomb is announced as probably 华阳太后's (beyond range, but a perfect coda).
- Source strength: 1990 + 1999 from 人民日报海外版 (via 中新网) [OK]; 1990 road link also in chinadaily govt page [OK]; 2006 from 陕西省政府英文网 snippet [SEC]; 1991 airport opening only Wikipedia [SEC]; 2018/2020-22/2025/2026 from 新华社/光明日报 [OK].
- Film logic: the white model's runway and the tomb's 外藏坑 sit on the same 咸阳原; "the road to the sky uncovered the road to the underworld".

### Rank 2 — 秦咸阳城 under the fields of 窑店 → 秦汉新城 / 国家考古遗址公园
- Spans: capital founded -350, imperial capital -221, burned -206 (史记 "火三月不灭"; 府库 excavations show 秦末 fire).
- Modern echoes (all dated, all [OK]): 1959 archaeology begins; 1988 national listing; 2011 秦汉新城 founded "而秦咸阳城遗址正在秦汉新城之中"; 2016-12 scandal "整个新城几乎就是覆盖在了秦都遗址上面"; 2016–19 府库 & "北宫乐府" 石磬; 2019 石铠甲作坊; 2021 六号高台 = 政务大殿 (荆轲刺秦 stage); 2022 保护规划; 2024-05 秦汉馆 opens "毗邻秦咸阳城遗址核心宫殿区"; 2025-06 国家考古遗址公园 (703 ha).
- Source strength: excellent (人民日报, 澎湃, 中新网, 中国日报). Weakness: no single "discovery day" like 金沙; the drama is slow erosion vs. protection (72 km² → "20余平方公里" protected).

### Rank 3 — 渭河: 咸阳古渡 → 咸阳湖 → bridges → metro crossing
- Spans: 秦汉 three Wei bridges (澎湃 2020: "秦汉时期，在咸阳一带的渭河上架起三座大桥"); 咸阳古渡 "秦中第一大渡" with burnt wooden piles still visible at low water (新华网 2022); Ming old town on the north bank (1369/1371).
- Modern echoes: 2004-10/2005-07/2007 咸阳湖 dams the river (地方志办 snippet); 2013-04-18 古渡遗址博物馆 [SEC]; 2017-07 咸阳湖二期; 古渡廊桥 (748 m, sxdaily/咸阳广电 2021); 2023-09-21 metro Line 1 ties the two banks' cities; 2026-01 渭河咸阳段 national "美丽河湖" case (陕西日报).
- Source strength: medium. 关中八景 attribution and the pile-discovery year are unverified; 咸阳湖 dates come from a gov page that could only be read as a snippet.

### Rank 4 — 后稷 at 武功/邰 → 杨凌 (1997)
- Spans: legend (《史记》"封弃于邰，号曰后稷"; 邰 "相传今陕西省咸阳市武功县西南"); 教稼台 monument [WEAK sources].
- Modern echo: 1934 国立西北农林专科学校 (新华每日电讯 2024); 1997-07-29 杨凌示范区, "1978年的杨陵还仅仅是陕西省武功县下属的一个镇" (人民日报 2019).
- Source strength: legend layer strong (人民日报 explicitly links 后稷 "教民稼穑" to 杨陵), monument weak, dates fine. Main problem: 40 km west of the film's bbox — needs a map zoom-out, so it works better as a prologue than as the anchor.

Recommendation: anchor on 汉阳陵/airport (Rank 1) with 秦咸阳城 (Rank 2) as the central "burned city" layer; use 渭河 as the continuous visual spine; use 后稷/尹家村 only in the opening Neolithic/legend beat.

## 5. UNVERIFIED ITEMS AND CORRECTIONS TO THE BRIEF

Corrections:
1. Metro: 森林公园站 is the Phase-2 terminus (opened 2019, in 沣东新城/西安 side). The 2023 Phase-3 extension runs 咸阳西站 → 森林公园站 (not incl.), 10.6 km. The Xianyang terminal station is 咸阳西站.
2. Railway: 陇海铁路 reached 西安 on 1934-12-27 (潼西段), 咸阳站 was built in 1936, and the 西宝段 opened to 宝鸡 on 1937-03-01. "1935" in the brief is the 西宝段 construction start (1935-01) / 西潼 completion (1935-02), not the arrival at Xianyang.

4. 西咸新区 "2017 西安代管": 2017-01 was 代管 of the new area / 托管 of 15 乡镇街道; 2021-07 the province issued 《关于西安市全面代管西咸新区的指导意见》 — list both.
