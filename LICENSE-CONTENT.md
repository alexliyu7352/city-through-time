The code of this project is under the MIT licence (`LICENSE`). The films and everything a city brings are under a different one:

**Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International (CC BY-SA 4.0)**, https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/

This covers, unless a file or a city's `CREDITS` says otherwise:

- every city folder under `cities/` (its `city.yaml`, `script.yaml`, `TREATMENT.md`, `FACTS.md`, `MUSIC.md`, `VOICE.md`, its research notes
  and the pictures made for it);
- the films rendered from them, and their web bundles;
- `cities.json` and the posters.

Material by others keeps its own licence, listed in each city's `CREDITS`: building and map data (CC BY 4.0, ODbL 1.0), footage and
photographs from Wikimedia Commons (CC BY-SA, CC BY, CC0, public domain), fonts (SIL Open Font License 1.1, in `fonts/`).

The films are ShareAlike because they include CC BY-SA footage: a film made with it is an adaptation and has to carry the same licence.
A new city contributed here comes under the same terms.

To credit a film: the city's name and *一城春秋 · A City Through Time*, its authors as listed in `cities.json`, a link to this
repository, and the licence.
